## 2. Purpose of the Role (Job Scope)

The role is responsible for:

1. **Technical execution**: running the ACHIEVE tools and processes, which include all the applications used in mapping. 
2. **GIS mapping**: village and church location data
3. **Survey implementation**: forms, rollout, data collection
4. **Data integrity**: accuracy and completeness of what enters the systems
5. **ACHIEVE-related analytics**: dashboards and reports

**Who it supports:** national reporting leadership, field teams, and COTW partners.
**Goal:** timely, accurate, high-quality survey and mapping processes, with ACHIEVE technical operations aligned to national reporting objectives.

## 3. Responsibilities by Theme

### A. Technical Implementation & System Management
- Manage and maintain user access for **iShare, Sparrow, and iMetrics**
- Troubleshoot system issues; escalate to **global ACHIEVE Tech** or **COTW tech representative** when needed
- Support onboarding of national partners and ministries, and migration of their data into **iShare**
- Assist with **COTW Tool Access Request Forms** and **Excel permission sheets**
- Analyze and prep survey/village/church datasets **before bulk import**
- Conduct GIS research to find better or alternative village data
- Maintain a **repository of GIS sources** for national datasets

### B. Survey & Mapping
- Train TTI and COTW leaders on surveys, mapping tools, and workflows
- Work with the National Reporting Coordinator on village survey data collection and data entry in iMetrics
- Prepare village survey forms and missing-village forms, ensuring proper formatting
- Verify village boundaries and datasets; research and update missing information
- Support training teams with **map-printing readiness** for TOTs

### C. Data Entry, Quality & Analytics
- Coordinate national data-entry processes, including **Data Entry Teams (DETs)** pre-launch and onsite at TOTs
- Ensure accurate church and survey data uploads in iMetrics
- Produce dashboards, analytics, and progress reports
- Identify missing villages and data gaps; coordinate corrections
- Train DET members on iMetrics, QC workflows, and Sparrow/Falcon. This involves training admins & FRs pre-cycle and during the cycle.
- Review survey forms and verify missing village data
- *Enforce the reporting sequence: initially it was Sparrow first, then iMetrics church entries. Right now, we can pause sync and then upload changes to iShare afterwards* 
- Produce national survey engagement reports and TOT reporting summaries
- Audit **red/yellow/green villages** and verify accuracy via site visits where applicable
  - Audit includes iShare audits, inventory audit, and iMetrics dashboard 

### D. Field Coordination & Training
- Attend TOTs for live technical support and training
- Train **Field Representatives (FRs)** on the iMetrics Mobile App for survey collection
- Support FR involvement in early survey efforts at priority locations
- Maintain consistent communication with field and reporting teams
- Give one-on-one support to **Pauls** during and after TOTs
- Conduct periodic **phone audits** and **case studies** to assess survey quality
- Attend in-country COTW Tech Meetings and/or MMTA Tech Meetings as needed

### E. Reporting & Communication
- Communicate promptly via email, phone, Teams, and WhatsApp
- Submit quarterly or monthly reporting metrics
- Collaborate regularly with the National Reporting Coordinator on survey alignment
- Give ministry leaders survey progress summaries
- Maintain shared folders and tracking systems for survey data and **missing village logs**

## 6. Key Workflows Implied

**Survey lifecycle**
1. Prepare village survey forms and missing-village forms
2. Verify village boundaries and GIS data
3. Print maps for TOTs
4. Train leaders, DETs, and FRs
5. Collect data (iMetrics Mobile App)
6. Data entry: **Sparrow, then iMetrics church entries**
7. QC, gap identification, and corrections
8. Audit (phone audits, case studies, site visits for red/yellow/green villages)
9. Dashboards, engagement reports, and progress summaries to leadership

**Access management loop**
Request (COTW Tool Access Request Form) → Excel permission sheet → grant/maintain access → troubleshoot → escalate to global ACHIEVE Tech or COTW tech rep when needed

## 12. Observations: Gaps and Things to Clarify

These are points the JD leaves open. They may be worth raising before signing or in the first weeks.

1. **"Full time" vs "contractual."** The header says full time; the acknowledgment says contractual. Clarify contract length, renewal terms, and benefits.
2. **Overtime and weekend work.** No mention of compensation, time off in lieu, or how much to expect.
3. **Performance appraisal.** Termination is tied to it, but the JD gives **no KPIs, targets, or appraisal criteria**. Ask what "meeting expectations" looks like (for example, data accuracy rate, report turnaround, number of trainings).
4. **Travel scope.** Frequency, duration, regions covered, and who covers costs are not stated.
5. **Reporting lines.** The role reports to the Regional Reporting Manager but works "in close collaboration" with the National Reporting Coordinator. Clarify who sets priorities when they conflict.
6. **Undefined acronyms and systems.** ACHIEVE, MMTA, COTW, Falcon, and Sparrow are not explained. Onboarding documentation will matter.
7. **Scope breadth.** One person covers GIS, survey ops, system admin, training, QC, analytics, and reporting. Ask what is prioritized in rollout vs. off-season periods, and what support exists (global ACHIEVE Tech, COTW tech).
8. **Site-visit audits.** "Where applicable" is vague; clarify criteria for when an on-site audit is required.
9. **Tool access dependencies.** Troubleshooting depends on escalation to global or COTW tech. Ask about expected response times so you are not blocked.
